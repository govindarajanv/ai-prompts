# Example: sales outreach (few-shot)

## How NOT to do it (before)
"I am writing to tell you about our new analytics suite. It has many features.
Please book a demo. Best regards."

## How to do it (after)
"Hi Priya, last spring you trialed our dashboard to track your team's usage of
the ops tooling. I noticed you left before the reporting module shipped — that
feature now gives you the exact numbers your PO asked for then. Want a 15-minute
walkthrough this week? Either way, happy to leave you a one-page summary."

## What changed
- Opened with a fact about the recipient (no generic intro).
- Referenced the prior trial and the specific feature that addresses their need.
- Ended with a low-commitment, specific next step.
